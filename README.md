# Pogo Stick

[Download Pogo Launcher.exe](https://github.com/zinuska2011-del/the-pogo-game/releases/latest/download/Pogo.Launcher.exe)

Open the launcher, select **Install game**, and sign in with your online account.

Already have an older launcher? Download this updated copy and replace your old launcher. Your account and installed game are kept. Incomplete installations offer **Repair game**; failed downloads offer **Retry download**.
